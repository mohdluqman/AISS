Our group works toward developing **safe, secure, responsible and socially aligned AI systems**, with a special focus on the Global South and South Asia. We examine how contemporary AI models produce systematic biases, misrepresent culturally diverse populations and overlook region-specific notions of fairness and harm.

We develop datasets, benchmarks, auditing methods and mitigation techniques to identify and reduce AI risks. Our broader goal is to help build AI that is technically reliable, culturally aware, inclusive and trustworthy across diverse societies.
