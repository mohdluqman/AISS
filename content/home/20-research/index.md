### AI safety and evaluation

Assessing model behaviour, failure modes and the reliability of AI systems.

### Bias, fairness and cultural context

Examining how AI represents people, languages and communities, particularly across South Asia and the Global South.

### Security and trustworthy systems

Developing methods for evaluating and improving the security and trustworthiness of intelligent systems.

### Benchmarks and mitigation

Creating datasets, benchmarks, audits and mitigation approaches that make model limitations measurable.
